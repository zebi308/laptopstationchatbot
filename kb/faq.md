## Q: How long is the warranty?
Every laptop comes with a 1 year comprehensive warranty, and you can extend it to two years from just £29. It covers standard wear and tear on keyboards, trackpads and hinges, manufacturing faults in parts such as the motherboard, ports and fans, and any hardware we installed as part of your order, such as RAM or storage upgrades. We also arrange and pay for the return of your laptop for diagnostics and repair. It does not cover accidental or deliberate damage (drops, cracks, water), screen damage, lines or dead pixels unless the laptop arrived faulty, software, or repairs and upgrades done by a third party. Batteries are a consumable item and are not covered. To claim, fill in the online booking form: we may fix common problems remotely, or arrange the return with a label. If a laptop can't be repaired, we replace it.
Source: https://www.laptopstation.co.uk/pages/our-warranty-information

## Q: Can I return a laptop?
Yes. You can cancel within 14 days of receiving your order without giving a reason, and you must tell us within that time. We also offer exchanges for unwanted items for up to 30 days from delivery, as long as the item is unused, in its original condition, and returned with all accessories, manuals and parts, and the original packaging where reasonably available. If the laptop is faulty, damaged or not as described, you have a right to reject it within 30 days of delivery, and we arrange or reimburse reasonable return costs. For an unwanted item you normally pay the return postage. To start a return, email help@laptopstation.co.uk or raise a support ticket in your account, and use a tracked service and keep your proof of postage. Refunds go back to your original payment method.
Source: https://www.laptopstation.co.uk/pages/returns-information

## Q: What do the condition ratings mean?
We don't use a letter grade. Each refurbished laptop is checked, cleaned and graded, and the screen, inside case, lid and underside each get their own condition description (for example Nearly mint or Lightly used), with photos where needed. Each product page shows these in its Condition Information section, and the overall condition is shown as Brand New, Refurbished and so on. Before sale we test key components, including that all keys work, that the battery holds charge, and that the webcam and microphone work.
Source: https://www.laptopstation.co.uk/blogs/articles/our-refurbishment-process

## Q: How do you refurbish your laptops?
Stock arrives at our Eastbourne premises and gets a visual check for damage or blemishes. If a unit needs upgrades it is taken apart and the parts are fitted. We install an up-to-date version of Windows, run the initial setup, and check for software and driver updates. We test key components, including that all keys work, that the battery holds charge, and that the webcam and microphone work. Then we clean and grade it. Every order is unboxed and checked again against a quality control form, and photos are taken.
Source: https://www.laptopstation.co.uk/blogs/articles/our-refurbishment-process

## Q: I placed an order before the changeover. Will it be fulfilled?
If your order was placed before 24th March 2025, it was under the previous ownership. While we'll do our best to help, please be aware we may have limited access to older order records and systems.

## Q: I contacted customer service before the transfer. Will someone get back to me?
We currently have limited visibility of past messages or cases submitted before the transfer. If your enquiry is still outstanding, we recommend contacting us again with as much detail as possible so we can assist you moving forward.

## Q: I want to return an item purchased before the transfer. Can I?
Items purchased before 24th March 2025 fall under the responsibility of the previous company. We're unable to process returns or refunds on their behalf, but we're happy to advise where we can.

## Q: I'm owed a refund from a previous order. What should I do?
If you're due a refund for an order placed before the change of ownership, we recommend reaching out to your payment provider or bank to request a chargeback.
